# Stakeholder acceptance of AI-mediated recruitment in Saudi higher education

Replication code for the empirical chapters of a doctoral thesis at King's Business School, King's College London.

The study surveyed academic staff, professional staff and leaders at eight Saudi universities. It combines attitudinal scales with a choice-based conjoint experiment on the design of AI recruitment systems.

| Folder | Chapter | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `stage1/` | Chapter 3 | Diversity and Inclusion Climate and Expected Procedural Justice in AI-Mediated Recruitment |
| `stage2/` | Chapter 4 | How Do Stakeholders Weigh Governance Against Efficiency in AI-Mediated Recruitment? |
| `stage3/` | Chapter 5 | Preference Variation Across Stakeholder Roles, University Sectors, and Expected Procedural Justice |
| `database/` | Chapters 3 to 5 | Relational SQLite database of the clean data of all three stages, with example queries |

Each folder has its own README with instructions for running the code.

## Data

The survey data are not included in this repository. Responses were collected anonymously under King's College London ethics approval MRSP-24/25-49508.
